What happens, step by step

  1. 1Your request arrives.
  2. 2Hub reads the tier and your extra settings.
  3. 3Hub loads your saved rules.
  4. 4Your settings and rules are combined.
  5. 5Hub lists the models you may use.
  6. 6Hub works out what kind of work this is.
  7. 7Hub keeps the same model for the chat, so it stays fast and cheap.
  8. 8Hub scores the models and picks the best.
  9. 9Hub sets the thinking level for the job.
  10. 10For planning and coding, a second model checks the answer.
  11. 11Answers stream to you as they come. Checked answers wait for the check.
  12. 12If a model fails, hub tries another allowed one.
  13. 13Hub records what it did. You can see it in the history.

Your rules win

Allow

Always lets a model in. It beats an exclude.

Exclude

Keeps a model out, unless an allow names it.

An env rule replaces everything else.
